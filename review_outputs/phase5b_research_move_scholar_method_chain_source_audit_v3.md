Authority: researcher-authorized, disabled development only

## Scope and evidence standard

This additive audit covers three function-only research moves:

- `Institutional Feedback Deepener`;
- `Robustness-Axis Switch`; and
- `Incentive-Implementation Stress Test`.

The audit distinguishes two claims that must not be conflated:

1. a move reconstructed from the scientific architecture of published papers;
2. a research habit explicitly described by the scholar in an official
   interview, lecture, autobiography, or method essay.

A successful paper is not by itself evidence of the author's private research
process. The audit therefore checked representative paper chains and available
official first-person material. It labels a move `paper-chain-derived` when the
functional pattern recurs across papers but is not explicitly claimed as a
general method by the authors. Only function-level content enters the corpus;
no persona, prose imitation, comprehensive intellectual biography, or named
scholar authority is created.

The v3 release preserves the exact v1 and v2 releases and adds five admitted
source cards. Context-only cross-checks are cited below but are not represented
as hash-bound corpus evidence.

## Representative-chain and experience cross-checks

These sources were inspected to avoid deriving a supposed scholar method from
one successful paper. They are contextual checks, not additional admitted
cards and not independent novelty evidence.

### Institutional feedback chain

- Acemoglu and Robinson,
  [A Theory of Political Transitions](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/aer.91.4.938),
  models how current political conflict changes future regimes and how future
  redistribution feeds back into current democratization and coup incentives.
- Acemoglu and Robinson,
  [De Facto Political Power and Institutional Persistence](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/000282806777212549),
  carries the same formal-versus-effective-power distinction into a compact
  persistence account.
- The existing v2
  [Acemoglu Nobel interview](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2024/acemoglu/1722488-interview-transcript/)
  supports reading, real problems, alternative approaches, and repeated error
  checking, but it does not explicitly state `Institutional Feedback
  Deepener` as a general method.
- The
  [James Robinson Nobel interview](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2024/robinson/1925411-interview-transcript/)
  confirms sustained curiosity, connection-finding, collaboration, and field
  engagement, but does not independently validate the formal move.

Adjudication: `Institutional Feedback Deepener` is
`paper-chain-derived`, not a claimed private method of either scholar. The
independent Doval-Skreta limited-commitment paper already audited in v1
supports the same current-information-to-future-design feedback in a different
economic domain. The shared-author Acemoglu-Robinson papers establish a
representative chain but do not count as independent positive anchors. The move
must explicitly solve future reoptimization; otherwise it collapses into an
ordinary representation change.

### Robustness chain

- Stanford's official publication records show a recurring line from
  [Robustness and Linear Contracts](https://economics.stanford.edu/publications/robustness-and-linear-contracts)
  to
  [Robustness and Separation in Multidimensional Screening](https://economics.stanford.edu/publications/robustness-and-separation-multidimensional-screening),
  robust contracting with additive noise, informationally robust trade, and
  robust auctions.
- The Bergemann-Morris program includes
  [Robust Implementation in Direct Mechanisms](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/robust-implementation-in-direct-mechanisms/),
  robust predictions across private information, and their
  [unified information-design perspective](https://elischolar.library.yale.edu/cowles-discussion-paper-series/2544/).

Adjudication: `Robustness-Axis Switch` is `paper-chain-derived`. The repeated
program supports changing one explicit knowledge or information axis and
tracking the surviving prediction, but no inspected first-person source
licenses generic worst-case modeling. The move therefore prohibits arbitrary
uncertainty sets and automatic max-min objectives. It is restricted to unknown
action/technology sets or private-information structures under fixed payoff,
timing, institution, and solution concept. The operator must be declared as
either a worst-case guarantee or an across-structure outcome set; those two
scientific objects cannot be merged.

### Incentive and implementation chain

- Myerson's
  [Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2007/myerson/lecture/)
  was checked against the published auction paper to confirm that explicit
  mechanisms, information, strategic behavior, and feasible design are the
  broader program rather than an auction-only notation choice.
- Maskin's
  [Nash Equilibrium and Welfare Optimality](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article-abstract/66/1/23/1666384)
  defines implementation by equality between desired and equilibrium outcome
  sets and gives a constructive result under stated conditions.
- Maskin's
  [Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/maskin_lecture.pdf)
  explicitly reverses ordinary institutional analysis: start from the desired
  outcome and determine whether and how a mechanism can attain it.

Adjudication: `Incentive-Implementation Stress Test` combines one explicit
research-history account with an independent published-paper reconstruction.
It searches for the first incentive, participation, commitment, feasibility,
or equilibrium failure; it is not a generic compliance checklist and does not
replace the normal implementation tournament.

## Move-level adjudication

### Institutional Feedback Deepener

Admitted for disabled development. It is triggered only when a nominally fixed
rule plausibly changes a future power, information, participation, or
commitment state that participants can affect. It returns an exact
current-rule-to-future-reoptimization chain, closes the feedback into current
incentives, and compares it with a fixed-state benchmark plus a full-commitment
benchmark when commitment is the proposed channel. It also returns a minimal
two-period witness. If the future state is truly fixed or the feedback requires
a different research object, the move is inapplicable.

### Robustness-Axis Switch

Admitted for disabled development. It removes or varies exactly one strong
designer-knowledge or private-information assumption, declares the admissible
uncertainty set, declares a worst-case-guarantee or across-structure-outcome-set
operator, and records which prediction survives or fails. It does not mix the
operators, vary preferences or an unrestricted environment, default to
worst-case optimization, enlarge every assumption simultaneously, invoke
empirical identification, or treat a wider outcome set as a better theory.

### Incentive-Implementation Stress Test

Admitted for disabled development. It takes a desired allocation, social goal,
or intuitive mechanism and searches for the smallest profitable deviation,
participation failure, commitment failure, infeasible transfer, or unintended
equilibrium. It separates failure of the proposed implementation from failure
of the economic question and does not claim global impossibility from one
witness.
